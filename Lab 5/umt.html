Our Background

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. Its history began as the Centre for Fisheries and Marine Science under Universiti Putra Malaysia. The centre provided facilities for students to gain practical experience and for lecturers to carry out research.

In June 1996, the institution became a branch campus known as Universiti Putra Malaysia Terengganu (UPMT). It later developed into Terengganu University College (KUT) and was renamed Kolej Universiti Sains dan Teknologi Malaysia (KUSTEM) in 2001. These changes marked important stages in the university's development.

On 1 February 2007, KUSTEM received full university status and took its present name, Universiti Malaysia Terengganu. Today, UMT is recognised for its focus on marine-related fields, science, technology and the sustainable management of natural resources.
